# STUDIO2U

## RECORDING CONSENT, AUTHORIZATION & USER RESPONSIBILITY AGREEMENT

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** September 3, 2026

### IMPORTANT — PLEASE READ BEFORE PARTICIPATING IN A RECORDING SESSION

Studio2U is a technology platform that facilitates connections between clients, artists, recording engineers, producers, and other independent service providers.

**Studio2U does not conduct the recording session, determine whether a recording is lawful, obtain consent on behalf of users, or act as an agent for any participant unless expressly stated in a separate written agreement.**

The individual who initiates, makes, directs, authorizes, or participates in a recording is responsible for ensuring that the recording and its subsequent use comply with applicable law.

---

## 1. MY REPRESENTATION

By accepting this Agreement, I represent and agree that:

I am responsible for determining whether I have the legal right to record any person, conversation, performance, voice, sound, or other communication.

I will obtain all notices, permissions, releases, and consents required by applicable federal, state, local, or other law before making or using a recording.

I understand that recording laws can vary depending upon the jurisdiction, location, participants, circumstances, and type of communication being recorded.

---

## 2. CONSENT OF PERSONS BEING RECORDED

Before recording another person, I will provide whatever notice and obtain whatever consent is legally required.

Where applicable, I will ensure that every person whose communication is being recorded has provided the legally required consent.

I will not rely on Studio2U to determine whether one-party consent, two-party consent, all-party consent, written consent, verbal consent, or another form of authorization is required.

If I am uncertain whether recording is lawful, I agree not to proceed until I have obtained appropriate legal advice or otherwise obtained the permissions required by applicable law.

---

## 3. NO SECRET OR UNLAWFUL RECORDING

I agree that I will not use Studio2U to facilitate:

* Unlawful audio recording;
* Unlawful interception of communications;
* Unlawful surveillance;
* Secret recording where prohibited by law;
* Recording conducted for an unlawful or tortious purpose;
* Harassment or intimidation through recording;
* Blackmail or extortion;
* Unauthorized publication or distribution of recordings;
* Unauthorized use of another person's voice or performance; or
* Any other activity that violates applicable law.

---

## 4. STUDIO2U IS NOT THE RECORDER

I acknowledge that Studio2U is a technology platform and marketplace.

Studio2U may facilitate a connection between me and another user or service provider.

The fact that a recording session was discovered, booked, scheduled, paid for, or communicated through Studio2U does **not** mean that Studio2U:

* Directed the recording;
* Authorized the recording;
* Determined that the recording was lawful;
* Obtained consent from participants;
* Guaranteed that consent was legally sufficient;
* Owns the resulting recording;
* Controls what occurs during the recording session; or
* Accepts responsibility for the independent conduct of the participants.

---

## 5. INDEPENDENT USER RESPONSIBILITY

I understand that I am solely responsible for my own actions.

If I initiate, request, direct, perform, participate in, authorize, possess, distribute, publish, sell, or otherwise use a recording, I am responsible for determining that my conduct is lawful.

I will not represent to another person that Studio2U has determined that my recording activity is legal.

---

## 6. RECORDINGS MADE DURING A BOOKED SESSION

A Studio2U booking does not automatically grant me ownership or permission to record every person present at the session.

I am responsible for identifying everyone who may be recorded and determining whether each person's participation and recording requires authorization.

Where applicable, I will obtain releases concerning:

* Voice;
* Performance;
* Likeness;
* Name;
* Image;
* Musical performance;
* Spoken material;
* Interviews;
* Background conversations;
* Copyrighted material; and
* Any other protected material.

---

## 7. LOCATION PERMISSION

I am responsible for ensuring that the location where the recording occurs permits the recording activity.

If a recording occurs at a residence, business, studio, hotel, vehicle, workplace, event, public venue, or other location, I am responsible for obtaining any permission required from the owner, operator, occupant, organizer, or other authorized party.

Studio2U does not represent that any particular location is legally authorized for recording.

---

## 8. RECORDING OWNERSHIP AND RIGHTS

Studio2U does not automatically acquire ownership of recordings created during a Studio2U session.

Ownership, licensing, copyright, publishing, master rights, performance rights, royalties, and other intellectual-property rights must be determined between the appropriate parties.

Users are responsible for obtaining whatever agreements are necessary concerning ownership and use of recordings.
