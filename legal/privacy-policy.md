## 1. WHAT STUDIO2U DOES

Studio2U operates a technology marketplace designed to connect artists, clients, recording engineers, producers, and other independent service providers.

Studio2U may facilitate:

* Account creation;
* User profiles;
* Discovery of service providers;
* Booking requests;
* Scheduling;
* Communications;
* Payments;
* Transaction records;
* Reviews;
* Customer support;
* Fraud prevention;
* Security;
* Platform administration; and
* Other marketplace functionality.

Studio2U is not necessarily the provider of the professional services arranged through the Platform.
