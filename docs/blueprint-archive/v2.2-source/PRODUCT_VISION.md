# Product Vision

## Positioning
Studio2U Label World lets independent artists and labels run a customizable virtual music company through specialized AI workers. It combines an expressive 3D management-game interface with actual music-business services, creative production, research, and oversight.

## Core experience
An artist opens their branded luxury hip-hop headquarters in Three.js. A large personalized gold record plaque anchors the studio; stylish blocky/designer-toy characters wearing distinctive clothing and chains occupy real workstations. The artist speaks to the Executive, assigns a release, and watches workers move and animate in response to actual task events. Outputs are downloadable and traceable. The experience remains useful without the 3D client open.

## Commercial scope
Multi-tenant from day one: label branding, themes, gold plaque, agents, outfits, catalog, projects, credentials, model providers, budgets, genres, discovery markets, social channels, and virtual rooms are tenant-specific. Initial testing may use one owner account, but data isolation is foundational.

## Art direction
Luxury hip-hop headquarters meets polished stylized music tycoon: charcoal/black, metallic gold, violet accents, premium computers, mixing desks, guitar, studio monitors, awards, framed covers, lounge furniture. Supplied character/environment images are reference authority. Use actual Blender models and GLB exports, not flat backdrops. Never imply a decorative in-app award is an official certification.

## World strategy
One connected modular headquarters with one polished, functional Main Studio Hub first. All key agents exist in the domain model; four primary visible agents first (Executive, A&R Researcher, Producer/Builder, Reviewer). Other departments can share stations or be represented through UI initially. Add Production, A&R, Rights/Publishing, Catalog, Distribution, Marketing, Gallery as loadable rooms later. Do not merge the entire building into one monolithic GLB.

## Product principles
Real work over theatrical simulation; proof and auditability; human control of consequential actions; modularity; platform-neutral domain services; portable 3D assets; tenant privacy; low-friction artist onboarding; honest integrations; measured performance.
